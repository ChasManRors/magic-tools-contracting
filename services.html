Our Services

From masonry to full-service contracting — foundations to finishing touches.

Magic Tools Contracting started with bricks, stones, blocks, and concrete — steps, chimneys, fireplaces, pathways, walls, and patios. We've since expanded into general contracting, painting & staining, and deck building, so we can take on more of your home improvement project from start to finish.

Brick & Stone

Brick, stone, and block construction and repair for walls, steps, and structural masonry.

Concrete

Concrete steps, walkways, and patios built to hold up through New England winters.

Chimneys & Fireplaces

Chimney repair and rebuilding, plus fireplace masonry inside and out.

Patios & Walkways

Custom patios and pathways in brick, stone, or concrete for your outdoor space.

Walls

Retaining and garden walls, built to hold up against grade, weather, and time.

Repointing & Waterproofing

Repointing and flashing to stop water damage before it starts, plus waterproofing.

Painting, Staining & Tile

Interior and exterior painting, staining, and tile installation to finish out any project.

General Contracting

Full-project management for renovations and additions — coordinating every trade so your project runs smoothly start to finish.

Decks

Custom deck design and construction, built to extend your living space outdoors.
