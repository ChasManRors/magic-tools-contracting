Welcome to Magic Tools Contracting

We got our start as Magic Tools Masonry, specializing in brick, block, and stone construction and repair. Word of mouth from those masonry jobs led to requests for more, so we've since grown into a full-service contractor — adding general contracting, painting & staining, and deck building alongside the masonry work that's still at our core.

From patios and retaining walls to full outdoor rebuilds and home improvement projects of every kind, we bring the same craftsmanship and reliability to every job.
