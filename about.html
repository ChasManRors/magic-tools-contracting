About Magic Tools Contracting

Local masonry craftsmanship, built on trust.

Who We Are

Magic Tools Contracting is a masonry contractor based in Arlington, Massachusetts, led by Luciano da Silva. We work directly with homeowners across Arlington and the Greater Boston area, bringing hands-on craftsmanship to every brick, stone, and concrete project we take on.

From small repairs to full outdoor rebuilds, we handle the work ourselves — no subcontractors, no middlemen — so every job gets the same level of care and attention to detail, start to finish.
